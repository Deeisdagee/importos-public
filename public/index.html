03 / BEFORE YOU COMMIT

A better estimate starts with better inputs.

01. Check the quote

Confirm the currency, quantity and Incoterms with your supplier. Avoid counting freight twice when it is already included in the goods price.

02. Confirm the bases

Obtain the correct classification, valuation basis and applicable duty and tax rates from official authorities or your customs broker. Enter those values above.

03. Allow for the extras

Add clearance, handling, inland transport and other known charges. Storage, inspections, exchange-rate changes and unquoted fees can change the final cost.

Every assumption stays visible.

Duty = duty base × duty rate. Import tax = tax base × tax rate. Cash outlay = goods + freight + insurance + other charges + duty + import tax. Per unit = cash outlay ÷ units. Values calculate at full precision and display to two decimal places.

Tax and duty bases are independent manual inputs. Update them when shipment values or rates change. This tool does not determine classification, exemptions, excise, recoverability or official customs values.
